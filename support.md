  <h1>Support</h1>
  <p class="updated">We read every message.</p>

  <p>Email <a href="mailto:hello@whizbangapps.dev">hello@whizbangapps.dev</a> and we'll get back to you. The quickest way is Settings → Contact Me in the app, which fills in your device, iOS version and app version for you.</p>

  <h2>Common questions</h2>

  <h3>Where are my wheels and lists stored?</h3>
  <p>Only on your device. Dibs has no account and no server. Deleting the app deletes your wheels and lists, and they do not move to a new phone on their own unless you restore a device backup.</p>

  <h3>How do I make an option more likely?</h3>
  <p>Edit the wheel and tap the weight on an option. A higher weight gives it a bigger slice and a better chance. You can also switch an option off without deleting it.</p>

  <h3>How do I stop the same name coming up twice?</h3>
  <p>Edit the wheel or list and set “After a pick” to Remove winner (wheels) or Remove picked (lists). Each pick then drops out of the draw until you reset it. Nothing is deleted: removed options stay saved and come back when you reset.</p>

  <h3>The finger chooser didn't pick anyone</h3>
  <p>It needs at least two fingers on the screen, held still until the countdown finishes. Lifting a finger early takes that person out of the round.</p>

  <h3>How do I remove the ads?</h3>
  <p>Open Settings and tap Remove Ads. It is a one-time purchase with nothing to cancel.</p>

  <h3>How do I restore my purchase?</h3>
  <p>Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.</p>
